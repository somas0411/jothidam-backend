# Glossary of report terms

The words used for the main astrological terms in every report language. Prediction templates (`data/predictions/*.json`), rule-book translations (`data/rulebook/i18n/*.json`), the Notes page (`notes_i18n.py`) and the name tables (`i18n.py`) use these forms; keep new text consistent with them.

Not reviewed by a native speaker in any language (Tamil included). Corrections are welcome: change the term here and in the files that use it.
